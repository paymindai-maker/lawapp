# Legal / CA / Company Secretary Website Color System

## Brand Direction

The visual system should communicate:
- trust
- authority
- professionalism
- financial seriousness
- institutional reliability

The recommended visual direction is:

> Modern Institutional Premium

The design language should feel closer to:
- law firms
- consulting companies
- financial institutions
- corporate advisory firms

Not:
- startup SaaS products
- fintech neon interfaces
- playful UI systems

---

# Recommended Typography

## Heading Fonts (Serif)

Recommended:
- Playfair Display
- Cormorant Garamond
- DM Serif Display

Purpose:
- authority
- institutional elegance
- premium legal feel

---

## Body Fonts (Sans Serif)

Recommended:
- Inter
- Manrope
- Plus Jakarta Sans

Purpose:
- readability
- modern clarity
- professional appearance

---

# Recommended UI Characteristics

## Cards
- subtle borders
- minimal shadows
- generous spacing

---

## Buttons
- rectangular or softly rounded
- avoid pill-shaped buttons

---

## Animations
- subtle transitions
- restrained motion
- smooth hover effects

---

# Avoid

Do not use:
- neon colors
- purple gradients
- excessive glassmorphism
- overly saturated blues
- playful SaaS aesthetics
- flashy animations

---
